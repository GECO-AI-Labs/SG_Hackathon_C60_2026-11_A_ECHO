SYNTHETIC SAMPLE. Written for the ECHO prototype.

Campaign: Cohort 62 intake
Window: January to February 2027
Channel: Upskill Today LinkedIn page (organic only)

Objective
Primary: drive applications to the Cohort 62 intake (campaign / lead generation). Measured by link clicks to the application page.
Secondary: keep the page visible to career switchers between intake campaigns (awareness).

Audience
Mid-career professionals (5 to 15 years of experience) in non-tech roles who are thinking about moving into IT. Fresh graduates from non-computing degrees.

Key messages
- You do not need a computer science degree to start.
- You are employed from Day 1, not after you graduate from the programme.
- You will work on real projects for large companies, with a mentor.
- Cohort learning: you are not switching careers alone.

Proof points we may use
- Awards listed on the company page.
- Stories from past cohorts (only with their written consent).
- Welcome posts for new cohorts.

Tone
Warm, direct, confident. Speak to the reader as a capable adult making a big decision. Avoid hype words ("revolutionary", "game-changing"), avoid guilt ("don't get left behind") and avoid more than three hashtags.

Mandatories
Every campaign post links to the application page. No salary promises. No claims about placement rates unless the number is approved by the programme director.

Suggested posts
1. "Is it too late to switch to tech?" myth-buster (avoid opening with the question itself).
2. A day in the life of a trainee in week 3.
3. Mentor spotlight.
4. Last call before applications close.
