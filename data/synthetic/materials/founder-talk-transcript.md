SYNTHETIC SAMPLE. A fictional talk excerpt written for the ECHO prototype, in the spirit of the brand. Not a real quote.

"People ask why we employ trainees on the first day, before they have written a line of production code. The honest answer is that it changes who applies. If you ask a forty-year-old operations manager with a family to quit and pay for a course, only the people who can afford the risk will come. If you offer a salary and a project at the end, you get the people who have the drive but not the safety net. Those are often the best engineers we place.

The second reason is that it makes us accountable. When we hire someone, their success is our cost. So we train for the stack our clients actually run, not for a syllabus. If a client moves to a new cloud platform, the next cohort learns it.

The third reason is local. We train local people for local businesses. A bank in Manila or a logistics firm in Singapore does not want to fly in talent forever. They want a pipeline. That is what a cohort is: a pipeline with names and faces.

What I tell every new cohort on day one is this: you were not hired because you already know Java. You were hired because you will."
