Tech Talent Accelerator (TTA) by Upskill Today

Who it is for
Mid-career professionals changing into tech, fresh graduates, and final-year students who want to start on real enterprise projects instead of toy exercises.

How it works
1. Apply and assess. Candidates complete an aptitude and motivation interview. No prior coding background is required for most tracks.
2. Train in a cohort. Each cohort learns together, full time, with trainers who have shipped enterprise systems. Tracks include Java full stack, data and AI, and cloud and DevOps.
3. Start with a job from Day 1. Trainees are employed from the first day and placed onto large-scale projects with Fortune 500 companies and local MNCs after training.
4. Grow on the project. Mentors stay with each placement and check in regularly during the first year.

Why it works
Companies get people trained for their actual stack. Trainees get paid work and a mentor instead of a certificate and a job search. Training happens locally, for local businesses, in Singapore, the Philippines, Indonesia, Malaysia and China.

Recognition
SBR Technology Excellence Awards 2026 (AI - IT Services). Company of Good Award 2026. Innovation Partner for External Talent award from Accenture Philippines (January 2025). SkillsFuture Employer Gold Award (October 2023).
